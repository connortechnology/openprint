Standard Size Brochures are listed as dropdowns, please choose custom from the dropdown list for custom sizes.

Brochures are normally printed on Gloss Txt and Euroart Silk Txt. Stock thickness ranges from 60lb upto 115lb text. If you need a sturdy brochure then you can print them on either Gloss Cover C2S or Euroart Silk Cover ranging from 7pt to 14pt.

Stocks thicker than 80lb text might have be to scored before they are folded depending on the design of the file.

Other stocks used for Brochures are:

1. Uncoated offset (uncoated finish)

2. Domtar Colour Opaque (different coloured papers)

3. Environment PC100 (100% recycled paper)
