Calendar printing - full colour




Most of the calendars we do are 28 pages (12 months plus 12 pictures plus cover).

The inside of the calendar is normally printed on either Gloss text or Euroart Silk text 100lb. The cover printed on a thicker stock like 10pt. Calendards normally require a hole drilled on the top.

Other stocks used for Inside are:

1. Uncoated Offset (uncoated paper)

2. Environment PC100 (100% recycled paper)

Other stocks used for the Cover are:

1. Cougar Cover (uncoated finish)

2. Euroart Silk Cover (smooth matte finish)

3. Tango (extra thick coated)

4. Environment Smooth Cover (100% recycled paper)
