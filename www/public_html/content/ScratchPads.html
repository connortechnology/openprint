Scratch pads are normally printed on uncoated offset paper printed one side, they usually come with a backer board. Most pads are in 50 to 100 sheets per pad

Handout pads are normally printed both sides on a Gloss text

Standards sizes are usually 4.25" x 5.5" and 8.5" x 5.5", multiple size options available.

There are no minimum quanity on pads
