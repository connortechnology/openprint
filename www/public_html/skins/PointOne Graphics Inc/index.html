POINTONE GRAPHICS INC.

Welcome

Our legacy in pre-press and printing, ISO certification, as well as an award for being one of Canada's fastest growing companies attests to our commitment to our customers - we're serious about printing!

PointOne's climate controlled pressroom boasts six late model presses including a Rotoman web press printing 60,000 sheets per hour, 2 perfector presses and 6 and 7 colour presses with in-line aqueous coating.

PointOne's 70,000 square foot building also houses a state-of-the-art prepress department & full bindery.

PointOne is also proud of its commitment to sustainable forests. A key part of PointOne's commitment to sustainable forests involves offering paper products bearing the recognized Program for the Endorsement of Forest Certification (PEFC) logo. These products are manufactured with wood fibers from certified well-managed and sustainable forests, according to PEFC standards. To maintain this commitment, we follow published PEFC Chain of Custody procedures. PointOne also holds FSC chain-of-custody certification. (SW-COC-002325) The FSC logo identifies products that contain wood from responsibly managed forests independently certified in accordance with the rules of the Forest Stewardship Council.

PointOne Graphics is a member of the DMA and DMAT.
