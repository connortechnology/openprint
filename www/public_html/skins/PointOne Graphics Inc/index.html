PointOne Graphics holds FSC chain-of-custody certification. (SW-COC-002325) The FSC logo identifies products that contain wood from responsibly managed forests independently certified in accordance with the rules of the Forest Stewardship Council.

PointOne Graphics is a member of the DMA, CMA, and DMAT.

Commitment to PEFC Chain-of-Custody Standards
The Senior Management at Point One Graphics is committed to implementing and maintaining our Program for the Endorsement of Forest Certification (PEFC) Chain-of-Custody requirements in accordance with the published PEFC standard. Our Chain of Custody procedures ensures that products purchased bearing the PEFC logo were manufactured using paper products who’s wood fibres came from certified well managed and sustainable forests. Details of these processes and procedures are available anytime by request from our Marketing Manager, Tanya Low, at tanyalow@point-one.com.
