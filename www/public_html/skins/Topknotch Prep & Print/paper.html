Paper information

We have a few basic house stocks which are cost effective and affordable plus we also carry a range of branded stocks.

House Stocks

  1. Gloss Text
    We carry a couple of house brands, stocks will vary depending on the thickness and size of the sheet required to print the order. Our house stocks consists of Riviera and Endurance.
  2. Gloss Cover C1S
    Our house brand for this is Highpoint. This stock is coated on the one side and uncoated on the second side. This stock is normally used for projects that need to have an image on the front and to be able to write on the back.
  3. Gloss Cover C2S
    Our house stock for this ranges depending on the thickness of stock and kind of work being printed on it. Our basic stocks are Riviera, Highpoint, Endurance and Carolina. This stock is used for business cards, postcards, pocket folders, door hangers, club flyer's, greeting cards, book covers, rack cards etc..
  4. Silk/Dull text
    Our house brand for this is Riviera. This is a low gloss/dull finish; which prints as good as a gloss finish but a paper that doesn't glare in the light and you can write on. This stock is used for brochures, magazines, flyer's, sell sheets, pamphlets etc..
  5. Silk/Dull Cover
    Our house brand for this is Endurance
  6. Uncoated Offset text
    Our house brand for this is Enova Offset Txt This is an uncoated sheet similar to bond paper but comes in different thicknesses. This has a complete matte finish and the print will look totally different than a coated paper, everything will get a bit darker and the ink will look flat. This stock is used for Letterheads, Newsletters, notepads, pamphlets that need to be written on etc..
  7. Crack and Peel (label stock)
    Our house stock is strata or mactac This is a label stock which comes in 4 different finished, uncoated, silk, semigloss and cast coated (super high gloss) This is used for printing high quality labels either digital or offset which can also be diecut.
