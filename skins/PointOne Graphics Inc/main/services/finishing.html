Finishing

Offline finishing services are another important component to the PointOne offering, and we've brought it in-house to provide our clients with one-stop service. Our folding and stitching lines can gather signatures, stitch booklets, and trim to finished size a variety of digests, magazines, catalogues and other multi-page printed products. Cutting, drilling, padding and shrink-wrapping services are also available.

We Match the efficiencies of our press lines with our bindery services to ensure we meet the demanding schedules of this industry.

  • 3 saddle stitchers
  • 7 folders
  • 3 cutters
  • Drilling & Shrink wrapping

For additional services such as Direct Mailing, Die Cutting, Coil, Wiro and Perfect Binding, pointOne has alliance partnerships with a variety of trade facilities and lettershops to ensure we can offer our clients complete end to end solutions.
