ABOUT POINTONE

The PointOne Difference

For over 15 years PointOne Graphics has built a solid reputation as one of Canada's leading print experts.

From our 70,000 square foot climate controlled production facility in Etobicoke, Ontario, we supply a wide range of print and finishing services. Our clients look to us for innovative products that are producted on time and on budget. Our state-of-the-art production equipment and automated workflow systems provide for consistent quality and are the foudnation to our ISO 9001 certification.

PointOne's Heidelberg press lines are modern, efficient and include a variety of sizes and options, ensuring we've got the right solution for each unique print project. From our 8 colour, 40" perfector press, which can print both sides of a sheet in one pass to the addition of our brand new 5 unit Man Roland Heat Set Web Press which can offer inline gluing, perfing and folding, PointOne Graphics continues to invest in the very latest technology.

An assortment of 6 colour and 4 colour presses, as well as finishing equipment for folding, stitching and gathering further adds to our abilities to meet demanding project deadlines.

"This in-house expertise means we're accountable for your complete project, from receipt of your art file to finished product, saving you both time and money."

Combined with industry leading prepress services including our fibre optic supported FTP site, the latest Computer To Plate Imaging solutions, CIPS3 computerized inking systems and online spectrometers to monitor ink densities, our ability to deliver consistent quality and a tighter dot is unsurpassed.

But printing excellence begins long before ink hits paper - it starts with PointOne's friendly, knowledgeable staff who are customer focused, and driven to meet your challenges. We've positioned everything and everyone in our organization to serve you better - hands-on, dedicated management, attentive customer service representatives, experienced prepress technicians, and an accountable production team - our goal is to earn your business with every project we product.

Add in our easy to use, online estimating tool, huge paper purchasing power, and the latest in printing technology, and one can see why PointOne is quickly becoming Canada's premier print production facility.

"PointOne Graphics... we're serious about print."
