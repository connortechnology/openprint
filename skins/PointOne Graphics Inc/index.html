Welcome

Our legacy in pre-press and printing, as well as an award for being one of Canada's fastest growing companies attests to our commitment to our customers - we're serious about printing!

PointOne Graphics Inc. climate controlled pressroom boasts five late model Heidelberg presses including an 8 colour perfector printing two sides at 10,000 sheets per hour, 6 colour presses with in-line aqueous coating and a 7 colour Heidelberg.

PointOne Graphics Inc. 70,000 square foot building also houses a state-of-the-art prepress department & full bindery.

PointOne Graphics holds FSC chain-of-custody certification. (SW-COC-002325) The FSC logo identifies products that contain wood from responsibly managed forests independently certified in accordance with the rules of the Forest Stewardship Council.

PointOne Graphics is a member of the DMA and DMAT.

PointOne's Commitment to Sustainable Forests
A key part of PointOne's commitment to sustainable forests involves offering paper products bearing the recognized Program for the Endorsement of Forest Certification (PEFC) logo. These products are manufactured with wood fibres from certified well-managed and sustainable forests, according to PEFC standards. To maintain this commitment, we follow published PEFC Chain of Custody procedures.
