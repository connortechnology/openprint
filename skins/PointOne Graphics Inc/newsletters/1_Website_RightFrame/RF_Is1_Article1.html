PointOne Announces 2% Price Cut

PointOne has cut its prices by 2 percent. The reduction, which took effect in late February, applies across-the-board to all PointOne services including prepress, printing and finishing.

Today’s tough economic times are affecting just about every business these days: our lower prices are designed to help ease the pressure. We’re also seeking to make it easier for you and your customers to maintain a strong marketing presence in a challenging market.

Give us a call and we’ll show you how our competitive price structure can stretch your print and advertising budget.
