How to Order:

Please follow our easy-to-use step-by-step guide:
  1. Select U.S. or Canadian currency by clicking on the corresponding flag icon.
  2. Choose the desired print service from the list of services at the left.
  3. Enter the order details to obtain an instant price, including shipping. Follow the directions on the page.
  4. Click on the ORDER button. Log in and enter the project details. If this is your first order, the system will guide you through a registration procedure, after which you may continue with your order. Then, authorize your order by clicking the AUTHORIZE button.
  5. Upload your file or files to be printed or upload them later.
  6. You will receive an order confirmation document by e-mail.
  7. We will invoice you by e-mail for the order total. The e-mail will have information for you to pay electronically by credit card. The payment transaction is through QuickBooks and is encrypted and totally secure. Once your payment is accepted your order will be put into production.
  8. We will send you a PDF proof of your files for your sign-off before your job goes to press.
  9. We will send the printed materials to you via UPS.
